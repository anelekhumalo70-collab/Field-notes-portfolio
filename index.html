04 / The author

More than a job title.

A little about the person behind the pages. The longer version is always a conversation away.

I’m a copywriter and creative strategist who likes getting underneath the obvious answer. I’m drawn to language, culture, human behaviour and the small details that reveal a much bigger picture.

I care about work that knows what it wants to say, why it matters, and who it is saying it to. Sometimes that means finding the strategy. Sometimes it means finding the sentence. Usually, it means asking better questions before doing either.

This site is part portfolio, part working notebook: a place for finished work, unfinished thinking and the connections between them.
